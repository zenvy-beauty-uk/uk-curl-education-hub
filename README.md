## 🌿 About Zenvy Beauty

**Zenvy Beauty** is the UK's specialist online retailer for **curly, coily, wavy and afro-textured hair**, founded by **Emma Rusby** in Teesside, United Kingdom.

We curate over **11 community-chosen brands** — including The Doux, SheaMoisture, Curlsmith, Oyin Handmade, Ecoslay, Camille Rose and Curl Keeper — all stocked in the UK, **duty-paid**, with **free UK delivery on orders over £25**.

No import duties. No US shipping costs. No compromising.

> *\"Built by a British curlie. For British curlies.\"*
